Research Question

How Does Changing the Slit Separation in a Double-Slit Experiment Affect the Interference Pattern on a Screen?

Hypothesis

As slit separation increases, fringe spacing will decrease.

This is because fringe spacing is inversely proportional to slit separation: doubling d will halve y.

y = λL / d
